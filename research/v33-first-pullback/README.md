# V33 First Pullback Reclaim

V33 is an independent research strategy. V31 remains available and unchanged.

The setup separates trend direction from entry timing:

- track a close beyond the previous 12 completed 15-minute candles;
- keep the setup for at most 12 candles and accept only the first pullback;
- require a close back through the previous candle within three candles;
- require current top/bottom-five rank, aligned closed 1H trend, no opposite closed 4H trend, volume ratio of at least 0.8, and no more than 0.8 ATR distance from the breakout level;
- use a structure stop with a minimum distance of 1.25 ATR, a 1.2R target, and a maximum 16-candle holding period.

The one-year check used 40 Bybit USDT perpetual proxies from 2025-09-02 through 2026-09-02. Account assumptions were 500 USDT, 5% planned risk per trade, a 10x gross cap, and no fees, slippage, or funding.

It did **not** meet the joint target of at least 65% win rate, 1.6 profit factor, and two account trades per day. It is presented in the site as a transparent research observation, not as a passed or production-ready strategy.
